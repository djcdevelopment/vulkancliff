- Every dense model: x6.6-6.9 at B=9, x3.5-3.7 at B=16 (70B dual: x3.5 / x1.9).
- MoE models gain only their dense-attention share (gpt-oss-20b x1.8, Qwen3-30B-A3B x1.1);

- Flat S_PP across B within each config confirms no memory spill contaminated any leg.
